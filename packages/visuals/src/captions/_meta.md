# Visual: Captions

## Description
Burnt-in captions for muted autoplay. Shows one "page" (≈ 60 characters, wrapping into ≤ 2 lines) at a time on a soft plate above the platform UI zone, fading in and out over ~4 frames, with the word being spoken coloured in the accent.

## Keywords
captions, subtitles, burnt-in, karaoke highlight, word timestamps, muted autoplay, safe zone, transcription

## Research and key prompts
- Timing must come from transcription of the rendered clip (whisper word timestamps), not from the TTS provider's estimate — estimates drift by hundreds of milliseconds.
- Paging belongs in the pipeline, not the component: the component only decides visibility and the current word, so it needs no captions dependency and stays deterministic.
- A short linger (default 180 ms) after a page's last word avoids flicker between pages; a 4-frame fade avoids hard pops.
- Default `bottom: 470` clears the ~420 px caption/controls zone of Reels and Shorts (`20-craft-reels.md`). Where a scene draws in that band, pass another `bottom` (the test-strip scene of the proof reel uses 720).
- Omit the component where the spoken line is the scene's own headline (hook, CTA) — it would duplicate the text and collide with the layout.
- Rendered with `zIndex: 1000`: a scene element with its own z-index once drew over the plate.
- Centred with `left/right: 0` + auto margins + `width: fit-content`: an absolutely positioned box at `left: 50%` shrink-to-fits into the 540 px right of its edge and wraps a 24-character line.
- The caption text is the **written** form from the script (`> subtitle:` line, falling back to the spoken prose with markers stripped); timings come from whisper, aligned by character position.
- Words marked `*like this*` in the script arrive as `emphasis: true` and stay bold and in the accent for the page's whole life — the reading emphasis survives muted autoplay even where the voice cannot carry it.
- Normally rendered through `<Voice scene subtitles subtitlesBottom />` from `audio/`; the direct component stays for pages from other sources.

## Technical approach
- Time in the clip = `(frame − offsetFrames) / fps`; the page whose `[startMs, endMs + linger)` contains it is shown.
- Words render as spans; the current word takes `colors.accent`; emphasised words take the accent and weight 700 throughout.
- Plate: `colors.background` at ~72 % alpha, radius 16.

## Style adaptation
- Text `fonts.body`, `foreground`; highlight `accent`; plate `background`.
- `fontSize` (default 44 ≥ the 40 px body minimum), `maxWidth`, `bottom`.

## Props
`pages` (`CaptionPage[]` from the voice index), `offsetFrames?`, `bottom?`, `fontSize?`, `maxWidth?`, `highlightCurrentWord?`, `lingerMs?`, `theme?`

## Data dependencies
Pages are generated by `packages/pipeline/voiceover` into the reel's `voice/generated.ts` (types in `audio/voice-types.ts`).
