# Open decisions (client)

Decisions that belong to the user and have been deferred. **Agents do not
resolve these on their own** — not as a proposal, not as a default. Do the
work that is independent of them and ask. When one is decided, move the row
to `80-memory-decisions.md` with the date. Harness-level open items live in
the harness `agents/rules/82-open-decisions.md`.
